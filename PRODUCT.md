# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in India planning a pilgrimage for themselves or their family, often booking on behalf of parents or a small group. Many are first-time visitors to a circuit and are deciding on a phone. They want to know when a group leaves, what it costs per person, what each day involves, how hard the walking is, and whether seats are left.

## Product Purpose

Darshan Yatra sells group pilgrimage tours run by its own team. Each yatra has a fixed itinerary, a set of departure dates, a per-person price, and a limited number of seats. Success is a traveller finding a suitable yatra and sending a booking request for a specific departure.

## Positioning

Fixed-departure group yatras with the departure dates, seat availability, per-person price, and day-by-day plan shown up front, before any phone call.

## Operating Context

- Circuits covered: Char Dham and Himalaya; Jyotirlingas across India; Gujarat and West; South India temples.
- Travel is seasonal. Himalayan shrines open roughly May to October; other circuits run most of the year.
- Travellers compare by departure city, duration, price, and physical difficulty.
- A booking request names one departure and a number of travellers and holds seats until the team confirms by phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- First version: home, yatra list with filters, yatra detail with itinerary and departures, booking request that reserves seats.
- No accounts, login, or online payment in the first version.
- Prices are per person in Indian rupees.
- Terminology: "yatra" for a tour, "departure" for a dated run of a yatra, "booking request" for the form submission, "darshan" for the temple visit.
- Undecided: cancellation and refund policy, payment method, operator contact details.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Darshan Yatra. No logo, colours, or typefaces have been fixed by the owner.

## Evidence on Hand

- No photography, testimonials, ratings, or customer numbers exist. None may be invented.
- Seed yatras, prices, and dates are illustrative catalogue data, not real commitments. Place names, distances, and season windows are real.

## Product Principles

1. Dates, price, and seats come first. A traveller should never have to ask for them.
2. Be honest about effort: altitude, walking distance, and age suitability are stated plainly.
3. Works for someone booking for their parents on a phone with a slow connection.
4. Say what is included and what is not.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Many travellers and the relatives they book for are older: generous text size, clear contrast, large tap targets, and no reliance on colour alone.

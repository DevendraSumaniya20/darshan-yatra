# Home and catalogue surfaces

Scope: home (Persuade), yatra list, yatra detail and booking request (Operate, inheriting the same world).

Audience and job: someone in India choosing a group pilgrimage for family, usually on a phone. They need the leaving date, seats left, price per person and the effort involved, then send a booking request for one departure.

Proof on hand: the live departure list with seats and prices. No photographs, testimonials or ratings exist.

Memorable moment: tear-off date-pad sheets with very large red numerals, one sheet per departure.

Unresolved: cancellation policy, payment method, phone number for the footer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a bazaar trade calendar whose date pad is live. Departures are pad sheets; the date is the biggest thing on the page. Refuses the photo hero with a search bar and a row of equal tour cards.

OWN-WORLD: Flat print inks at page scale: chrome yellow plate, ultramarine bands, vermilion numerals and rules, white pad paper, black text. Block-shaded sign lettering (Yatra One) for names; Anek Latin for text and numerals. Double printed frame, perforated sheet tops, square corners. Full departures are torn off to a stub.

STORY: Fixed dates exist, seats are limited, the price is known. Pick a sheet, read the plan, request seats.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ultramarine header band with yellow wordmark. Yellow plate inside a red and blue frame: shaded headline left-aligned at display scale, one line of support, then a row of the next departures as pad sheets, each a link, with "See all dates" as the primary button beside the headline.

FORM: Bazaar calendar print with tear-off date pad; candidate 7 of 7; seed key bd49cea9. Raises: a strict shared column grid across every page (from the man-machine grid); one unbroken vermilion thread through the day-by-day itinerary (from the provenance ribbon).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
